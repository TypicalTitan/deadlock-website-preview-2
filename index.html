Bracket

Every team gets a second chance. Lose a series and you drop to the lower bracket; lose a second and you're out.

Schedule

No set start time. Teams book their own games inside a 1-week window per round. All times are Pacific.

  1. Round 1: both upper semis
  2. Round 2: upper final and lower round 1
  3. Round 3: lower final
  4. Round 4: grand final

One game per day per series, so a Bo3 takes 2 or 3 days. Different series can play at the same time. If a series hasn't booked by day 3 of its window, the organizers pick the time.

Teams

Four teams of six, plus subs. Make a team or ask to join one on the sign-up page. There's no seeding: an organizer randomly draws the bracket order.

Rules

The short version. The full rulebook has everything.

The draft

  1. Coin flip for sides: Hidden King or Archmother.
  2. Both teams ban 3 heroes, revealed at the same time.
  3. Second coin flip: the winner takes the first pick.
  4. Picks go 1, 3, 3, 2, 2, 1. The other team gets the first triple pick and the last pick.

A hero can be on only one team per game. Pick turns have no time limit.

Lanes

Right after pick 12, teams talk separately for 2 minutes, then tell the organizer which pair takes York, Broadway and Greenwich. Both lane sheets get revealed at the same moment. Once the game starts, anyone can swap.

Match day

  • Pauses: up to 5 minutes per team, per game.
  • Remakes: disconnect or wrong hero in the first 2 minutes restarts with the same draft and lanes.
  • Short a player: play 5v6, ask for a bot, or take the forfeit.
  • No-shows: 15 minutes of grace, then it's a forfeit.
  • Patches: live patch. A hero released mid-event stays banned until the next round.
  • Exploits: no deliberate bug abuse.

Rulings

The organizer's call is final, including anything the rulebook doesn't cover. Appeals only happen if both teams agree a ruling is unfair. Not sure about a rule? Ask an organizer before your match.

Organizers
